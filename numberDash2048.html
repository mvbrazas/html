HOW TO PLAY

Run smart. Jump clean.

  1. Use Left and Right, the arrow keys, or horizontal swipes to move across three lanes.
  2. Pass through the bright green ×2 gate to double your number. Missing a gate leaves your number unchanged.
  3. Jump with the Jump button, Space, Up Arrow, or an upward swipe to clear crates. A crate hit halves your number and costs one life.
  4. Three crate hits end the run. Reach the finish with at least the level target to win and earn 500 finish points.
  5. A completed rewarded ad can restore one life and continue the same run, up to twice per session.
