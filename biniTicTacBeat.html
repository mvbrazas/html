HOW TO PLAY

Three in a row. Right on the beat.

  • Place your flower in an empty cell. Three in a row wins.
  • Every move earns 100 points. The optional visual 100 BPM guide adds Good +25, Great +50 or Perfect +100.
  • The BINI Eight and Beat Challenge power tile doubles move points. Fast wins add a bonus; consecutive wins grow your streak multiplier.
  • Complete all eight BINI Eight boards for a 2,000 point Bloom Master bonus.
  • Chill is unpredictable, Groove blocks and takes wins, and Slay uses optimal strategy.
  • Beat Challenge uses the BINI track for its timed run. If the track is unavailable, play Quick Play or BINI Eight as unranked practice.
