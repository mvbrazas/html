HOW TO PLAY

Make room for red.

  1. Tap a car to select it. Use the large arrows, keyboard arrows, or drag it along its long side.
  2. Each bay moved costs one move. You cannot drive through another car or outside the lot.
  3. Move every car out of red's path to the right. Red then drives out automatically, without spending a move.
  4. Undo refunds the exact number of bays moved. Hint and Reset are free. Levels 1 and 2 are fixed practice; later levels have changing layouts.
  5. Three failed lots in a row end the session. A completed rewarded ad can add one move to the same lot, up to twice per session.
