HOW TO PLAY

Make it a sweet streak.

Hold your mouse button and drag, or swipe with your finger through candies and gummies. Each sweet counts once.

Build combos for up to 2.5× points. Sugar Rush sections double your points. A missed sweet resets your combo. Dark, spiky candies cost 500 points and reset your streak.

Easy moves slower; Hard brings more sweets and hazards. Finish the 60-second challenge to save your score.
