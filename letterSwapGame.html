HOW TO PLAY

A little word-juggling.

  1. Tap a tile, then a tile directly above, below, left, or right to swap. Diagonals do not count.
  2. Words appear horizontally or vertically. Select a Claim button to score them.
  3. Find all three target words within 30 swaps. Each word scores once; bonus words remain available until claimed.
  4. Three consecutive swaps that form no new word end the round. A rewarded continue clears that streak and adds five swaps.
